Dear Dr. Smith,

I have been following your work on fitting Active Inference to empirical data — especially the 2022 tutorial with Friston and Whyte, and the 2026 complexity-matched comparison with Chou, Hakimi, and Hsu. That comparison makes a point I have been trying to sharpen from the design side: overlapping predictive accuracy is not the same as a theory-specific information-seeking mechanism.

I developed a simulation-tested experimental design that addresses a structural identifiability problem in Active Inference. In the preprint I report that standard symmetric information-seeking tasks can allow a flexible curiosity model to become nearly observationally equivalent to an exact-information-gain controller, and that matched asymmetric observation channels can restore discrimination against that tested curiosity family while holding prior, mean cue accuracy, and instrumental sampling value constant. The public repository currently reproduces the shipped v1 and Nelson checks; the broader v4–v8 adversarial sequence is documented in the preprint but is not yet packaged for end-to-end public reproduction.

This is not a claim of a new design class. Nelson et al. (2010, Experiment 3, Condition 1) already tied probability gain while varying information gain and found only 12/22 participants preferred the higher-IG feature after experience-based learning. The live question is therefore whether an Active Inference decision framing adds anything empirically distinct beyond that older OED contrast. A further equivalence analysis limits even a later positive result: it would support exact-MI-sensitive valuation relative to specified alternatives; it would not uniquely validate Active Inference or the Free Energy Principle. The reduced controller is an EFE-derived epistemic-value component under stated assumptions, not a process-level implementation of full AIF.

Because a close human precedent already exists, I am **not** asking first for a recruitment slot. The ordered next gate is EXP-002: locate or reconstruct Nelson raw / supplementary materials and reanalyse the IG-vs-PG contrast under an AIF-framed reading. Only if that gate keeps the question informative would I then seek a small feasibility pilot (learnability of asymmetric contingencies, heterogeneity, heuristic substitution). I would contribute the design, computational models, and analysis pipeline; critical comments and pointers toward Nelson materials would already be a valuable response.

Would this framing fit your group’s current interests well enough for a short conversation?
