# Research programme map (positioning, not v6 evidence)

Epistemic ID is one kill-test inside a broader FEP/AIF programme. It does not replace the other tests.

| Slot | Question | Role of this paper |
|---|---|---|
| Epistemic-value identifiability | Can exact information gain be distinguished from specified information-seeking alternatives? | **This note.** Synthetic sequence v1–v7; human feasibility still pending. |
| Closed-loop causal intervention | Does a preregistered generative AIF model beat named controls on out-of-sample responses to new interventions? | Out of scope. Different estimand (causal/predictive of interventions). |
| Coarse-graining / scale-gap | Do variational/Markov-blanket properties survive cell → tissue → organism? | Out of scope. Theoretical. |
| Clinical replication | Do precision / information-seeking accounts replicate in independent cohorts? | Out of scope. |
| Process-level discrimination | Do RT, confidence, or neural measures split AIF process theory from same-objective alternatives? | Named as a future route around the v7 ceiling; no predictions claimed here. |

The FEP-audit file that motivated this map is **not** empirical support for v6, does not close hierarchical v9, and does not replace the Nelson 2010 prior-art finding.
